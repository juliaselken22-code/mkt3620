Coach Custom Bag

Coach Custom Bag

The Coach Custom Bag is a product concept that my team developed for a marketing project. The idea focuses on giving customers the ability to personalize their handbag through interchangeable pieces and different style options.

Our goal was to create a product that combines the recognizable Coach brand with personalization, creativity, and self-expression.

Target Audience

Girls Ages 12–16

The primary target audience for the Coach Custom Bag is girls between the ages of 12 and 16 who are interested in fashion, trends, social media, and expressing their individual style.

This audience may want a recognizable brand while also wanting a product that feels unique and personal.

Product Features

Interchangeable Panels

Different panels can be switched out to change the appearance of the bag without purchasing an entirely new handbag.

Personalization

Customers can select different styles and designs that match their personality and preferences.

Coach Branding

The product keeps the recognizable style and branding of Coach while introducing a younger and more customizable experience.

Customer Benefits

More Variety

Customers can create multiple looks from one handbag by switching the customizable pieces.

Self-Expression

The customizable design allows customers to create a bag that reflects their personal style.

Longer Product Use

Instead of replacing the entire bag when trends change, customers could update individual pieces and give the bag a new look.

Marketing Strategy

Because the product is targeted toward a younger audience, social media would be an important part of the marketing strategy.

Social Media Marketing

Platforms such as TikTok and Instagram could be used to show customers different ways to customize and style their bags.

Influencer Marketing

Fashion and lifestyle creators could demonstrate different combinations and encourage customers to create their own designs.

Personalization Campaign

Marketing could focus on creativity and self-expression by encouraging customers to design a bag that feels unique to them.
